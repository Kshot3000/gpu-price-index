Methodology & sources

How each column is built

What the badges mean

  • LIVE — fetched from the OctaSpace marketplace API in your browser just now.
  • few listings — fewer than 5 listings; the average can swing on a single provider's price.
  • verify live — the average sits far below every competitor; likely a mispriced or test listing. Check the marketplace before counting on it.
  • snapshot — a published list price, verified on the date shown. Marketplaces move; treat as a reference, not a quote.

What's excluded

  • Platform fees, storage, and egress — excluded from all columns equally.
  • Vast.ai's column shows the cheapest "from" offers on its spot (interruptible) tier — the lowest prices Vast.ai publishes, so this comparison gives Vast.ai its best case. Spot instances can be preempted; standard on-demand rates run higher. OctaSpace rentals bill per minute and are not preempted.
  • SaladCloud's column shows official "from" rates on the Lowest priority tier — the cheapest of four tiers (High, Medium, Low, Lowest); lower tiers can be preempted by higher-priority workloads, so this gives SaladCloud its best case too. Per-second billing while instances run.
  • OctaSpace's A100 row is the 40 GB SXM4 variant; competitor A100 rows are 80 GB cards.

GPU Price Index is an independent community project. Not affiliated with OctaSpace, Vast.ai, RunPod, or SaladCloud. Prices are references, not quotes — always confirm on the provider's site before renting.
